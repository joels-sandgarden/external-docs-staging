Use this page when Google Drive supplies background material for Doc Holiday.

## What connecting Google Drive does

Connecting Google Drive adds a read-only Source that Doc Holiday uses for background material while it writes. It does not start new work on its own.

## How to connect Google Drive

1. Open **Sources**, select **Add Source**, and choose **Google Drive**.
2. Enter a **Source Name**.
3. Follow [Google Drive authentication instructions](https://doc.holiday/docs/connections/#google-drive) to prepare the service account and finish credential setup before uploading the JSON.
4. Paste the downloaded JSON contents into **Service Account JSON**, or upload the JSON file with **Upload**.
5. Select **Save Changes**.

## Verify

Open **Sources** and confirm the Google Drive Source shows **Healthy**.
