Open **Settings → Billing** to review the available plans and what each one includes. The page shows the current billing options for the organization. Admins on the Bring Your Own Key and Open Source plans can access it; Enterprise billing is handled with the account team.

![The Billing page with the three plan cards](/screenshots/billing-and-plans/tier-cards.png)

## Open Source Plan

Our Open Source Plan is free for public open-source projects and runs on the organization's own provider key for inference. See [Add a key](./b6-add-your-openai-key.md) and [AI provider keys](./h3-ai-provider-keys.md) for setup context.

The Apply dialog asks for project name, public repo URL, and description. Doc Holiday sends the decision by email.

## Bring Your Own Key

Bring Your Own Key costs $100/month. Eligible organizations can start with a free trial, and billing stays under the organization's account portal.

If billing is not current, Doc Holiday pauses gated flows until the subscription is restored.

Bring Your Own Key eligibility depends on customer type. The Billing page shows the plan only when the organization qualifies.

Use [Add a key](./b6-add-your-openai-key.md) and [AI provider keys](./h3-ai-provider-keys.md) for key setup and management.
