Open **Settings → Billing** to see the available plans, trial status, and billing messages. The page shows three plan cards for the current billing options and is available to admins on the Bring Your Own Key and Open Source plans; Enterprise billing is handled with the account team.

![The Billing page with the three plan cards](/screenshots/billing-and-plans/tier-cards.png)

## Open Source Plan

Our Open Source Plan is free for public open source projects and uses your own AI provider key for inference. See [Add Your AI Provider Key](./b6-add-your-openai-key.md) and [AI provider keys](./h3-ai-provider-keys.md) for setup context.

The Apply dialog asks for project name, public repo URL, and description. After submission, a superuser reviews the application and emails the decision.

## Bring Your Own Key

Bring Your Own Key costs $100/month and includes a free trial. The billing portal manages the subscription.

The billing page shows when the trial is close to ending so teams can update billing before new work stops.

If the subscription lapses, Doc Holiday pauses new work until billing is current again. The banner reads: "Your BYOK subscription is no longer active. You can still view Doc Holiday, but new requests are disabled until you resubscribe."

Use [Add Your AI Provider Key](./b6-add-your-openai-key.md) and [AI provider keys](./h3-ai-provider-keys.md) for provider setup and management.
